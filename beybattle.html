Controls & timing tips

Blades move on their own. Dash: Q. Guard: E. Special: Space. To steer, hold the arena or use WASD / arrow keys.

Guard blocks 75% damage. Guard just as a hit lands for a perfect block: 90% blocked, +12 energy, and a counter-dash. Tap Counter within 1.8 seconds; a landed hit adds 3 damage.
