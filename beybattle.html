THE BASICS

Timing beats button-mashing.

Your blade moves on its own. Reduce your rival’s spin to zero. Win two rounds to take the duel.

➤ Dash Q

Rush toward your rival. After a perfect guard, tap Counter within 1.8 seconds: the next hit adds 3 damage.

◇ Guard E

Blocks 75% damage. Guard just as a rush hits for a perfect block: 90% blocked, +12 energy and a fresh counter-dash.

✦ Special Space

Energy builds over time and in clashes. Tap once when the meter is full.
